Once upon a time a poor boy named Jack lived with his mother in a little cottage at the edge of a dry, dusty field. All they owned was an old cow called Milky-White, and one day she stopped giving milk.

Jack's mother sent him to market to sell the cow, but on the way he met a strange man who offered him five magic beans for her. Jack said yes. When he got home his mother was so cross that she threw the beans out of the window and sent him to bed without supper.

The next morning Jack looked out and saw an enormous beanstalk growing where the beans had landed. It was so tall that it disappeared into the clouds. Jack climbed and climbed until he reached a land above the clouds, where a huge castle stood.

Inside the castle lived a giant. "Fee-fi-fo-fum!" he roared. Jack hid until the giant fell asleep, then grabbed a hen that laid golden eggs and ran all the way back down the beanstalk. He chopped it down with an axe, and the giant came crashing to the ground.

Jack and his mother were never poor again.
