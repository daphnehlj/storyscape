Three little pigs left home to build houses of their own.

The first pig built a house of straw. The second pig built a house of sticks. The third pig worked hard all day and built a strong house of bricks.

A big bad wolf came to the straw house. "Little pig, little pig, let me come in!" He huffed and he puffed and he blew the house down, and the first pig ran to his brother's house of sticks. The wolf huffed and puffed and blew that house down too, and both pigs ran to the house of bricks.

The wolf huffed and puffed and puffed and huffed, but he could not blow the brick house down. He tried to climb down the chimney, but the pigs had a pot of boiling water waiting, and the wolf ran away and never came back.
