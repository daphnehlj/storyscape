There was once a little girl who always wore a red hood that her grandmother had made for her, so everyone called her Little Red Riding Hood.

One day her mother gave her a basket of cakes to take to her grandmother, who lived in a small house on the other side of the forest. "Stay on the path," said her mother.

In the forest Little Red Riding Hood met a wolf. He asked her where she was going, and she told him. The wolf ran ahead by a shorter way, crept into the grandmother's house and hid her in the cupboard. Then he put on her nightcap and got into her bed.

When Little Red Riding Hood arrived she said, "Grandmother, what big eyes you have!" and "What big teeth you have!" The wolf jumped out of bed, but a woodcutter who was passing heard the noise, rushed in and chased the wolf away. Grandmother climbed out of the cupboard and they all ate the cakes together.
